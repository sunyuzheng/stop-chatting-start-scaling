# Key Learnings

## What Made The Deck Stronger

- The deck worked better once the argument stayed thesis-first instead of explanation-first.
- The strongest framing is not “AI is smarter now.” It is “the work method changed.”
- Demo slides land better when they prove compounding, not speed.
- The course section works better as a compact bridge, not a long sales block.

## What The Audience Should Remember

- Chat improves local tasks.
- Agents change the operating model.
- The difference comes from loops, context, and accumulated assets.
- The real upside is not speed. It is capability expansion and compounding.

## Why The Closing Structure Works

- `Curriculum` shows the capability ladder.
- `Methods` proves that each step has a concrete technical method behind it.
- `Closing` keeps the CTA simple: course for the full path, community for free support and examples.

## Practical Publishing Note

If you keep iterating after sharing, preserve these constraints:

- one idea per slide
- short speaker-friendly wording
- no dense course marketing block
- every framework slide must map cleanly to the core thesis
